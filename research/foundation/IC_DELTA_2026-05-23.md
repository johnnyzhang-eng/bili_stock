### Implication — the verdict was half artifact, half uncontrolled

The verdict reported `+13.91%/yr excess` for "avoid smart cubes" vs baseline. The empirical post-fix delta:

- top_smart_decile +11.22% vs no_smart_random +23.95% → **−12.73%/yr** for top vs out-of-pool.

That's almost the verdict's number, post-fix. So **the held-vs-not-held selection effect is NOT artifact** — it survives the rolling-skill / clean-mask / clean-fwd_ret pipeline. **What WAS artifact** is the IC framing: the verdict reported this delta as `IC = −0.0164, t = −4.97`, implying cross-sectional rank skill. After mask fix, IC = −0.0002, t = −0.08. **There is no rank skill; there is a binary in-pool-vs-out-of-pool delta.**

### Why this is still not validated alpha

The +13-22%/yr delta is uncontrolled for:

1. **Size**: smart cubes likely concentrate in large/mid-cap (where "smart money" attention focuses). Out-of-pool skews to small/micro-cap. 2024-2026 was a strong small-cap regime (CSI1000 +30% in 2024). The +22pp test-period delta is the smoking gun for size beta dominating.
2. **Liquidity**: out-of-pool includes very-low-volume names where execution slippage is much higher than 56bp; that drag is unmodeled.
3. **Industry**: smart cubes may avoid certain sectors that beat over the period.

These are precisely **B3-iii** (`no_smart_random` not size/liq matched, Codex finding). Until that fix lands or A1 is rerun through `foundation.Backtest(random_control=True)` with `Universe.broad/small_cap` + `min_turnover_20d`, the delta can NOT be claimed as alpha — only as a size/liq-confounded difference.

### Revised verdict line

- The verdict's `IC = −0.0164, t = −4.97` is **invalid** (rank-mask bug).
- The verdict's `+13.91%/yr excess` is **a real number but does NOT imply alpha** — it is a held-vs-not-held difference uncontrolled for size/liq exposure.
- A1 cannot be either VALIDATED or REJECTED until the comparison runs in foundation framework with size/liq-matched random control.
