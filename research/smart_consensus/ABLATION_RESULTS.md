## Attribution Readout

- The `2022+` original reconstruction is the direct comparison to the original A1 verdict and reproduces the IC closely (`-0.0161/t=-4.76` vs reported `-0.0164/t=-4.97`).
- B3-i is the largest isolated IC nuke in the verdict window: `Only B3-i` moves IC t from `-4.76` to about `-0.52`.
- B1 is the second largest isolated IC nuke: `Only B1` moves IC t to about `-1.85`.
- B2, B3-ii, and B5-i do not explain the IC collapse by themselves in this ablation; they remain methodology blockers because they fix timestamp honesty, universe integrity, and delisted/stale return treatment.
- The no-smart avoidance spread remains positive after IC fixes unless B3-iii matching is applied. Liquidity+board matching cuts the `2022+` avoidance spread from about `+11.0%/yr` to about `+5.7%/yr`; market-cap matching remains pending on a stable point-in-time mcap panel.


## Notes

- `Rank excess ann` is top-decile rank signal minus same-eligible-pool random.
- `Avoid excess ann` is random no-smart/out-pool minus random all-universe baseline; this is closest to the original +14% avoidance claim.
- `All fixes + B3-iii` uses liquidity-decile + board matched random for the avoidance baseline. Market-cap matching is deferred until the fundamentals/stock_data rebuild provides a stable point-in-time mcap panel.
- `Only B1` uses the existing `rolling_ann_gain.csv`; that rolling NAV builder already filters ETF/CB in NAV construction, so it is not a mathematically perfect isolation of B1 from B3-ii. The signal-side CB filter remains off in that row.
